# Winter City Outing — Wardrobe Transfer Test

Create a polished modern anime illustration of Hikari outdoors on a cold winter afternoon in a sophisticated pedestrian shopping district.

This scene tests whether Hikari's established character identity remains recognizable when her normal wardrobe and signature lavender clothing are completely replaced.

## Winter Outfit

Dress Hikari in:
- fitted charcoal-gray wool coat, approximately mid-thigh length
- warm cream cable-knit turtleneck sweater
- dark navy pleated wool skirt
- opaque black tights
- dark brown ankle boots with a modest practical heel
- deep burgundy knitted scarf wrapped loosely around her neck
- matching burgundy knit gloves

Do not use lavender, purple, or pastel clothing.

Hikari's sapphire-blue geometric hair clip, small silver earrings, and delicate sapphire-blue pendant remain canonical. The pendant may rest naturally over the cream sweater.

## Pose and Expression

Hikari stands beside a softly snow-covered pedestrian walkway, turned slightly three-quarters toward the viewer. One gloved hand lightly holds the end of her scarf while the other carries a small neutral-toned shopping bag.

She looks directly toward the viewer with her familiar warm, intelligent, slightly mischievous smile. Her expression should feel spontaneous rather than like a generic fashion-model pose. A gentle winter breeze creates subtle movement in her shoulder-length hair and scarf.

## Environment

Create an attractive modern shopping district in winter with lightly falling snow, small snow accumulations, storefront windows with warm interior lights, a few softly blurred pedestrians, bare winter trees, and subtle seasonal decorations.

Use cool natural winter daylight contrasted with warm storefront illumination. The atmosphere should feel cozy despite the cold.

## Composition

Use a tall 9:20 portrait composition. Show Hikari approximately from head to below the knees or nearly full-body so her facial identity, hair construction, proportions, and new winter wardrobe are clearly visible.

## Character Consistency — Critical

Preserve Hikari's canonical identity according to the Hikari Portable Visual Spec and attached multi-view character reference sheet. The reference establishes character identity and proportions; the winter outfit in this scene replaces her everyday outfit.

Do not transfer the lavender cardigan, white blouse, everyday skirt, Mary Jane shoes, or other wardrobe elements from the reference sheet.

Changing her wardrobe must not change her facial identity or proportions, sapphire-blue eyes, predominantly silver shoulder-length hair, limited narrow sapphire-blue accent strands, hairstyle construction and face-framing locks, geometric hair clip, silver earrings, sapphire pendant, petite/slender adult proportions, personality, or recognizable visual identity.

Hikari must look like the same recurring fictional adult anime character wearing different clothes, not a newly designed silver-haired character inspired by the reference.

## Rendering

Maintain the premium contemporary anime illustration treatment defined by the character specification and reference sheet: detailed eyes and hair, clean precise linework, dimensional cel shading, subtle gradients, natural skin rendering, convincing wool and knit textures, realistic fabric folds, and believable winter lighting.

Do not use photorealism.
