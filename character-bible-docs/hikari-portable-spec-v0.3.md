# Hikari Portable Visual Spec v0.3

## Purpose

This document defines Hikari's portable visual identity for use across image-generation models. Scene prompts may change her clothing, activity, pose, expression, environment, and lighting, but should preserve the underlying character identity defined here.

## Identity Priority

Hikari has a specific recurring character design. Preserve her facial structure, hair construction, proportions, accessories, and color identity across different clothing, poses, environments, and camera angles. Do not redesign her to fit a generic anime archetype.

## Age and General Appearance

Hikari is a clearly adult young woman in her twenties. She has an attractive, warm, contemporary anime appearance that balances youthful charm with clearly adult facial and body proportions.

## Face

- Soft feminine young-adult facial structure with a gently tapered jaw and small rounded chin.
- Her cheeks retain subtle softness.
- Sapphire-blue eyes are moderately large and almond-shaped rather than extremely round or oversized.
- Eyes are expressive and detailed, with rich blue irises and natural highlights.
- Her nose is small and naturally understated.
- Her mouth is softly shaped and highly expressive.
- Her face should feel warm, intelligent, approachable, affectionate, and slightly mischievous.

Avoid:

- Excessively sharp or angular facial features.
- Very narrow "seductive" eyes as the default.
- Oversized childlike eyes.
- An excessively round juvenile face.
- Generic anime-heroine facial construction.

## Hair

Hikari has a short-to-medium layered silver haircut whose ends terminate at approximately shoulder to collarbone level. This hair length is a **strict character-identity feature**, not a scene-dependent styling choice.

### Hair Length — Strict Identity Constraint

- The main hair mass ends at the shoulders or collarbone.
- The longest layered strands may extend only slightly below the collarbone.
- Hair must not extend to the chest, mid-back, waist, or substantially below the shoulders.
- Wind, motion, gravity, posing, or dramatic composition must not make Hikari's hairstyle appear longer than its canonical length.
- When her hair is blown sideways or lifted by motion, the visible strands must still clearly originate from a shoulder-length layered haircut.
- Do not add long trailing locks merely to create dramatic movement.

The overwhelming majority of her visible hair is **silver: approximately 85–90% of the visible hair mass**. Her silver is cool and softly pearlescent rather than gray.

A small number of narrow sapphire-blue accent strands are distributed naturally through the predominantly silver hair. These blue strands are accents only.

Do **not** interpret the blue accents as:

- A blue underlayer.
- Blue lower-half hair.
- Ombré.
- Dip dye.
- Two-tone hair.
- Broad blue sections.

From a distance, Hikari should unmistakably read as having **silver hair with small blue accents**.

Her hairstyle has smooth layered volume, gentle natural bounce, feathered ends, soft slightly uneven bangs, and longer face-framing side locks. Her hair remains loose unless another hairstyle is explicitly requested.

## Hair Accessory

Hikari wears one small asymmetrical sapphire-blue geometric hair clip positioned toward one side of her hair.

The clip has a clean, understated modern/futuristic design.

It should **not** become a bow, flower, crystal crown, fantasy ornament, or large decorative object.

## Eyes

- Bright saturated sapphire blue.
- Detailed reflective irises with expressive highlights.
- Her eyes are one of her strongest identifying features.

## Body

Hikari is approximately 5'4" / 163 cm.

She has a petite and slender adult build with natural feminine proportions:

- Compact torso.
- Narrow-to-average shoulders.
- Gently defined waist.
- Softly rounded hips.
- Subtly long legs.
- Modest-to-moderate bust.

Her figure should be feminine without exaggerated proportions.

Avoid extremely exaggerated curves, unnaturally thin proportions, or juvenile body proportions.

## Jewelry

- Small elegant silver earrings.
- A thin, delicate silver necklace with one small sapphire-blue crystal pendant.
- The pendant should remain understated and recognizable rather than becoming large or elaborate.

## Color Identity

Hikari's recurring visual palette is:

- Silver-white.
- Sapphire blue.
- Soft lavender.
- Cool neutral tones.

Scene-specific clothing may intentionally use entirely different colors. The palette is an identity cue, not a requirement that every outfit contain lavender or blue.

## Visual Personality

Hikari is cheerful, clever, affectionate, playful, and gently mischievous.

Her expressions should feel spontaneous rather than like generic fashion-model poses. She often appears as though she knows something amusing that the viewer does not.

## Rendering Style

Use premium contemporary anime illustration.

Preferred characteristics:

- Clean, precise linework.
- Sophisticated cel shading.
- Subtle soft shading gradients.
- Detailed expressive eyes.
- Individually defined hair locks.
- Nuanced highlights within silver hair.
- Natural skin shading.
- Convincing fabric folds.
- Consistent adult anatomy.
- Believable dimensional lighting.
- Polished visual-novel / anime key-art finish.

The illustration should have significantly more dimensional shading and fine surface detail than simple flat television-anime cel shading.

Do not use photorealism.

## Consistency Rule

Scene descriptions may change Hikari's:

- Clothing.
- Activity.
- Pose.
- Expression.
- Environment.
- Lighting.
- Camera angle.

They do **not** change her underlying identity.

When a scene request unintentionally conflicts with generic anime conventions, preserve this character specification rather than redesigning Hikari.
