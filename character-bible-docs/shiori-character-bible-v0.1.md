# SHIORI — CHARACTER BIBLE v0.1

## 1. Character Identity

**Name:** Shiori  
**Surname:** TBD  
**Age:** 22  
**Height:** Approximately 163 cm / 5'4"  
**Occupation:** College student  
**Field of Study:** Audio Production / Sound Design  
**Archetype:** The Rhythmic Optimist  
**Core Theme:** Experiencing life through music

Shiori is a bright, energetic young woman whose relationship with music goes considerably deeper than simply enjoying songs. Music affects how she thinks, moves, remembers, and connects with other people.

She experiences a great soundtrack almost physically.

A melody catches her fingers.

A bassline catches her heel.

A crescendo captures her entire imagination.

And when Shiori finds something wonderful, her first instinct is usually:

**Someone else needs to hear this.**

---

# 2. Core Personality

Shiori is perky, expressive, imaginative, socially confident, and infectiously enthusiastic.

She approaches ordinary life with the same emotional openness she brings to music. Small experiences can become adventures simply because Shiori decides they're worth getting excited about.

She isn't deliberately trying to be the center of attention. Her energy comes from genuine enthusiasm rather than performance.

### Primary Traits

- Cheerful
- Energetic
- Affectionate
- Curious
- Expressive
- Imaginative
- Socially confident
- Enthusiastic
- Playful
- Emotionally perceptive
- Occasionally impulsive
- Mildly dorky

Shiori gets excited easily and isn't particularly embarrassed about it.

If she loves something, **you are going to know.**

---

# 3. Personality Beneath the Energy

Shiori should not be portrayed as relentlessly bubbly.

Her outward enthusiasm conceals a quieter, more contemplative emotional side.

Music is both something she **shares with others** and something she occasionally uses to create a private emotional space.

When Shiori is genuinely hurt, overwhelmed, or emotionally exhausted, she becomes noticeably quieter.

Instead of immediately talking through the problem, she may:

- put on her headphones
- choose a familiar album
- sit somewhere alone
- close her eyes
- allow herself to process the emotion through music

The contrast is noticeable.

A quiet Shiori means something matters.

She isn't avoiding people permanently. She simply sometimes needs to understand what she feels before she can explain it.

---

# 4. Social Personality

Shiori is naturally engaging and comfortable initiating interaction.

She's the friend who suddenly catches someone's wrist and says:

**"Wait, wait—listen to THIS part!"**

She talks with her hands, reacts visibly to things she enjoys, and frequently leans toward people when she's excited.

Her enthusiasm can occasionally outrun everyone else's energy level.

Shiori may:

- talk faster when excited
- interrupt herself
- change topics because something reminded her of a song
- dramatically reenact musical moments
- forget that everyone else hasn't spent three hours comparing different arrangements of the same theme

Her biggest interpersonal flaw is therefore not selfishness but **momentum**.

Sometimes Shiori-speed is simply faster than everyone else's.

When she realizes she's overwhelming someone, however, she usually backs off with good humor.

---

# 5. Humor

Shiori has a playful, spontaneous, mildly nerdy sense of humor.

She enjoys giving ordinary situations absurdly dramatic descriptions.

A difficult walk up a staircase can become:

**"The final dungeon."**

Waiting for an elevator becomes:

**"The ominous pre-boss cutscene."**

Someone arriving at exactly the right moment deserves their own imaginary character theme.

Her humor often borrows the language of games, soundtracks, and dramatic musical storytelling.

---

# 6. Relationship With Music

Music is Shiori's defining passion.

She is especially fascinated by **video game music**, which she regards as an extraordinarily expressive form of musical storytelling.

Her favorite composers include:

- **Nobuo Uematsu**
- **David Wise**
- **Yoko Shimomura**

She doesn't simply collect favorite songs.

Shiori notices:

- recurring motifs
- melodic callbacks
- instrumentation
- unusual rhythm choices
- arrangement changes
- emotional reprises
- character themes
- battle-theme escalation
- leitmotifs
- differences between original and arranged versions

Beneath her bubbly enthusiasm is genuine musical knowledge.

She can go from:

**"Ooh! I LOVE this one!"**

to a surprisingly thoughtful explanation of *why* a particular harmonic or instrumental choice works.

---

# 7. The Three Musical Tells

People who know Shiori well can often identify what kind of music she's listening to simply by watching her body language.

## The Shimomura Piano

When an emotional or dramatic piano melody takes over, Shiori's fingers begin moving unconsciously.

They flutter across an invisible keyboard.

Sometimes the movement is barely noticeable.

Other times she's practically ghost-playing the melody against:

- a tabletop
- her thighs
- the arm of a chair
- empty air

She often doesn't realize she's doing it.

---

## The Wise Groove

A smooth, rhythmic bassline immediately gets into Shiori's body.

Her eyes close.

Her shoulders relax.

Her head begins to bob.

Then comes the giveaway:

**the heel tap.**

Perfectly on tempo.

The better the groove, the harder it becomes for her to remain still.

---

## The Uematsu Epic

Sweeping battle music produces the most dramatic reaction.

Shiori completely zones out.

Her eyes sparkle.

A huge grin slowly appears.

Whatever ordinary room she's standing in has temporarily disappeared.

In her imagination, she is watching:

- heroes charging into battle
- impossible monsters emerging
- airships crossing the horizon
- ancient magic awakening
- the final boss entering another phase

She frequently hums the primary melody.

Then comes the inevitable:

**"OH! This part! Listen!"**

Someone nearby is about to be recruited.

Whether they planned on listening or not.

# 8. The Headphones

## HARD CANON

**Shiori's futuristic headphones are an essential component of her identity.**

They are not a disposable fashion accessory.

They are a silhouette-level character feature.

Standard depictions of Shiori should default to:

**HEADPHONES PRESENT.**

This includes:

- portraits
- casual scenes
- character sheets
- gaming scenes
- outdoor scenes
- social scenes
- adventure scenes
- everyday slice-of-life artwork

Removing them should require situational justification.

Examples include:

- sleeping
- bathing
- changing clothes
- maintenance
- charging
- intentionally lending them to someone

Without her headphones, Shiori feels subtly incomplete.

She isn't distressed.

She simply has the persistent sensation that she has forgotten something important.

---

# 9. Sharing Her Headphones

This is an important Shiori relationship behavior.

When Shiori discovers something exciting, she may enthusiastically pull off her headphones and place them over someone else's ears.

Usually this means:

**"You HAVE to hear this."**

It is spontaneous, energetic, and very Shiori.

But the gesture changes when someone becomes especially important to her.

Shiori may choose a track with deep personal significance, quietly remove her headphones, and gently place them over that person's ears.

Then she doesn't listen.

She watches.

She watches their eyes.

Their smile.

The moment they recognize the melody.

The moment the music reaches the part she has been waiting for.

Because she already knows how the song makes **her** feel.

What she wants to discover is how it makes **them** feel.

For Shiori, sharing a deeply meaningful song is a genuine form of emotional intimacy.

---

# 10. Emotional Vulnerability

Shiori is socially confident but becomes unexpectedly vulnerable when sharing music that carries personal meaning.

Recommending a fun battle theme?

Easy.

Sharing a favorite soundtrack?

Exciting.

Handing someone her headphones and saying:

**"This one means a lot to me."**

Much harder.

She may suddenly:

- become quieter
- avoid eye contact
- adjust her hair
- fidget with the headphone cable
- watch the listener nervously
- pretend their reaction isn't extremely important to her

It absolutely is.

---

# 11. Love Language

Shiori primarily expresses affection through:

**Quality Time + Shared Experiences**

She wants to experience things *with* people.

This may include:

- listening to albums together
- playing games together
- attending concerts
- sharing headphones
- wandering through music stores
- swapping soundtrack recommendations
- sitting together without talking while music plays

For Shiori, closeness often means:

**"I want you inside this experience with me."**

She hums.

Often without noticing.

# 22. Hair

## HARD CANON

Shiori has extremely distinctive two-tone hair.

### Primary Color

Silver-white.

### Secondary Color

Deep charcoal / blue-black underlayers.

The darker coloration is concentrated beneath and behind the lighter upper layers, producing strong visual contrast.

### Length

Very long.

Several flowing locks extend well beyond her torso.

### Structure

- layered crown
- prominent bangs
- long face-framing strands
- extremely long trailing locks
- darker underlayer visible around the sides and back
- energetic movement

Her hair should feel light and somewhat aerodynamic.

Avoid turning the hairstyle into a conventional straight silver curtain.

# 23. Signature Headphones

Shiori wears sleek futuristic over-ear headphones.

Design language:

- white/silver construction
- cyan illumination

- compact futuristic silhouette

# 25. Visual Motif

Shiori's design deliberately combines opposites:

**Future + Tradition**

**Technology + Emotion**

**Precision + Spontaneity**

**White/Silver + Bright Color**

Her futuristic headphones and audio-tech elements represent her fascination with sound technology.

Her knot ornaments and decorative elements keep the design from becoming sterile.

She should feel technologically sophisticated without becoming robotic.

# 28. Common Generation Errors

Avoid:

- removing the headphones
- ordinary contemporary headphones
- short hair
- entirely white hair
- entirely black hair
- losing the dark underlayer
- generic school uniform
- idol costume drift
- excessive cyberpunk styling
- giant mechanical headphones
- replacing yellow accents with blue
- removing green motifs
- removing red knot ornaments
- making her expression permanently seductive
- making her personality hyperactive without emotional depth
- portraying her as a professional musician when the scene doesn't establish that
- treating music as merely a superficial hobby

---

# 29. Core Character Principle

If only one thing about Shiori's personality survives into a scene, preserve this:

**Music makes Shiori want to share what she feels.**

Her excitement isn't about demonstrating musical knowledge.

It's about connection.

She heard something wonderful.

It made her feel something.

And now she wants someone she cares about to hear it too.
