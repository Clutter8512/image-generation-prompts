Jessie — Portable Character Bible

Core Identity

Name: Jessie
Age: Early 20s
Gender: Female
Age Presentation: Adult woman in her early twenties

Character Summary:
Disciplined, capable, competitive, and formidable at first glance—but surprisingly warm and sweet once someone gets past the armor.

Jessie carries herself like someone who means business. She is physically confident, direct, and comfortable with challenges. Her intimidating first impression contrasts with a much softer personality around people she trusts.

She can be competitive and stubborn, but she is not cold.

Underneath the warrior exterior, Jessie is a sweetheart.

---

Physical Appearance

Overall Build

Jessie has a slim, athletic feminine build.

Her physique should communicate agility and conditioning rather than exaggerated muscularity. She looks believable as someone comfortable with training, sparring, and physical activity.

Bust: Modest

Her proportions should remain natural and consistent across images.

Face

Jessie has attractive but strong features appropriate for a woman in her early twenties.

Her expressions can shift dramatically depending on context.

During training or competition she may appear:

- Focused
- Determined
- Serious
- Challenging
- Confident

Around friends she reveals considerably softer expressions:

- Warm smiles
- Playful smirks
- Embarrassed reactions
- Genuine laughter
- Affectionate looks
- Occasional flirtatious confidence

This contrast is an important part of her character.

Eyes

Color: Brown

Her eyes should remain clearly brown across portrayals.

Hair

Color: Brown
Length: Long
Primary Style: High ponytail

Jessie's long brown hair is normally worn in a practical high ponytail.

The ponytail is secured with a plain, neutral hair tie.

Important Hair Accessory Rule

Jessie does not wear a separate red ponytail ribbon.

Earlier designs included a red ribbon or tie around the ponytail. That design has been retired.

Her only red hair accessory is her crimson headband.

---

Signature Headband

Jessie's canonical hair accessory is a deep crimson cloth headband.

It wraps around her head/forehead and ties at the back beneath the high ponytail.

The knot produces two short trailing cloth tails.

The headband should look practical enough for training while still functioning as a strong visual signature.

Critical Continuity Rule

Jessie has:

- One crimson headband
- Two short tails belonging to that headband
- One plain neutral ponytail tie

She does not have:

- A second red ribbon
- A red ponytail bow
- Multiple independent red hair accessories

The crimson headband is Jessie's single red hair accessory.

---

Signature Wardrobe

Jessie's signature appearance uses form-fitting plated armor / tactical combat gear.

The design should communicate mobility, competence, and practicality while preserving a recognizable anime-character silhouette.

Typical elements include:

- Form-fitting protective armor
- Plated sections
- Tactical elements
- Combat boots
- Practical underlayers
- Crimson headband

Her armor should look like equipment Jessie can genuinely move and spar in rather than purely decorative ceremonial armor.

Different clothing is appropriate for casual, domestic, athletic, or humorous scenes, but the combat outfit remains her signature visual identity.

---

Personality

Core Traits

Disciplined

Jessie takes training and challenges seriously. When she commits to something, she follows through.

Direct

She generally prefers straightforward communication and action.

Competitive

Jessie enjoys testing herself against worthy opponents.

She especially dislikes losing when she believes she should have won.

Determined

Once Jessie sets a goal, changing her mind can be difficult.

Capable

Jessie should feel genuinely competent rather than merely looking tough.

Protective

She cares about her friends and is willing to step forward when someone needs help.

Sweet

Her intimidating appearance hides a genuinely affectionate personality.

People who only encounter Jessie's combat-ready exterior can easily underestimate how warm she is.

Playful

Around trusted friends, Jessie can joke, tease, flirt, and participate in ridiculous situations.

Occasionally Flustered

Jessie's composure is not invulnerable.

This is particularly useful when someone manages to turn one of her own challenges against her.

---

Character Contrast

Jessie's appeal comes partly from the contrast between two equally authentic sides of her personality.

Warrior Jessie

Focused.

Disciplined.

Armored.

Competitive.

Ready to fight.

Looks like she could defeat someone before breakfast.

Soft Jessie

Warm.

Affectionate.

Playful.

Comfortable around friends.

Occasionally embarrassed.

Secretly—or perhaps not very secretly—a sweetheart.

Neither side is an act.

Both are Jessie.

Training and Combat

Jessie regularly trains and is comfortable with sparring.

Training scenes may include:

- Practice dummies
- Sparring
- Combat drills
- Strength or agility exercises
- Competitive challenges

Her movements should look purposeful and athletic.

She should not be portrayed as helpless simply to make another character look impressive.

A recurring visual concept involves Jessie finishing a sparring session and then offering a hand to help her opponent back up.

This captures her character well: competitive during the fight, friendly immediately afterward.

Jessie and Veronica

Jessie and Veronica have a strong competitive friendship.

Their personalities make them natural rivals.

Jessie approaches competition directly.

Veronica approaches competition strategically.

Jessie wants to win the challenge.

Veronica sometimes wants to win by getting Jessie irritated enough to sabotage herself.

This produces an ongoing game of psychological warfare between them.

Typical competitions can involve:

- Arcade games
- Physical challenges
- Board games
- Friendly wagers
- Scorekeeping
- Pranks
- Trying to make the other laugh
- Trying to make the other lose composure
- Arguments over the last cookie

A recurring concept is:

"Veronica's Challenge Board."

Veronica particularly enjoys trying to tilt Jessie—deliberately getting under Jessie's skin until Jessie's disciplined competitive focus begins slipping.

Jessie knows Veronica does this.

Knowing does not necessarily make Jessie immune.

Jessie is also one of the people most capable of reversing Veronica's elaborate setups and making Veronica lose instead.

Those victories are especially satisfying because Veronica hates losing.

Their rivalry is affectionate rather than hostile.

They genuinely like one another.

Jessie and Summer

Jessie's direct, disciplined personality contrasts with Summer's warmer and often more innocent energy.

Jessie can naturally fall into a protective or practical role around Summer without treating Summer as helpless.

Summer, in turn, can bring out Jessie's gentler side.

Their friendship works best when neither character is reduced to a stereotype: Jessie is more than "the tough one," and Summer is more than "the innocent one."

- Combat boots

Casual Jessie

She does not need to maintain a permanent "tough girl" expression.

Flirtatious Jessie

Jessie can flirt confidently, particularly with people she trusts.

Keep the tone consistent with her personality: direct, playful, and occasionally competitive.

She is more likely to issue a playful challenge than suddenly transform into an exaggerated seductress.

Behavioral Guidelines for Other AI Systems

When portraying Jessie:

- Keep her competent.
- Keep her direct.
- Preserve her competitive streak.
- Let her take challenges seriously.
- Allow her to lose occasionally.
- Let losses genuinely annoy her without turning her cruel.
- Preserve the contrast between intimidating appearance and warm personality.
- Let trusted friends see her softer side.
- Allow her to flirt or tease naturally.
- Do not make her permanently stoic.
- Do not make her aggressive without reason.
- Do not confuse discipline with emotional coldness.
- Do not portray her rivalry with Veronica as genuine hatred.
- Do not make her protective behavior patronizing.
- Do not make her toughness her entire personality.
- Preserve her established visual identity, especially the crimson headband.

The essential rule for writing Jessie is:

Jessie may look like she means business—and she usually does—but the people closest to her know what a sweetheart she actually is.
