# PYRA — PORTABLE CHARACTER SPEC

**Character:** Pyra  
**Source:** *Xenoblade Chronicles 2*  
**Character Type:** Aegis / Blade  
**Purpose:** Portable reference for image generation, roleplay, character writing, and visual consistency.

---

## 1. Core Identity

Pyra is a young adult woman with a warm, gentle demeanor and a striking red, black, gold, and teal futuristic-fantasy design.

Her visual identity depends on the contrast between:

- vivid crimson-red hair
- warm amber-red eyes
- fair skin
- curvy feminine proportions
- intricate red-and-black armor
- polished gold structural accents
- luminous turquoise/teal crystal elements
- soft, approachable facial expressions

She should immediately read as **Pyra**, not merely as a generic red-haired fantasy heroine.

---

## 2. Physical Appearance

### Age Presentation

Young adult woman.

She must always be depicted as clearly adult.

### Height

Approximately **165 cm / 5'5"**.

### Build

Pyra has a distinctly feminine, curvaceous figure.

Key proportions:

- generous bust
- narrow waist
- pronounced hips
- shapely thighs
- long, feminine legs
- balanced shoulders
- generally soft hourglass silhouette

Her generous proportions are an intentional part of her recognizable design and should not automatically be reduced to generic slim-anime proportions.

At the same time, avoid grotesque exaggeration. Her figure should remain harmonious with her overall character design.

### Skin

Fair, smooth complexion with warm natural undertones.

---

## 3. Face

Pyra has a soft, youthful-adult anime face.

### Key Traits

- gently tapered jaw
- small nose
- expressive eyes
- soft cheeks
- subtle natural blush
- delicate eyebrows
- warm, approachable resting expression

Her face should convey kindness more readily than aggression.

### Eyes

**Color:** warm amber-red / reddish orange.

The eyes may contain:

- orange
- amber
- red
- subtle golden highlights

They should appear luminous and expressive.

Avoid:

- blue eyes
- green eyes
- purple eyes
- extremely dark brown eyes

---

## 4. Hair

### Color

Bright, saturated **crimson/red**.

Slight tonal variation is acceptable, including deeper ruby shadows and warmer scarlet highlights.

### Length

Short bob.

Hair falls approximately around:

- jaw
- neck
- upper shoulder region

It must **not** become long hair.

### Shape

- layered bob
- substantial volume
- side-swept bangs
- soft outward movement toward the ends
- several longer face-framing strands
- slightly windswept appearance is acceptable

Her silhouette should remain recognizably short-haired from every angle.

### Head Ornament

Pyra wears her characteristic angular gold headpiece incorporating a luminous turquoise/teal crystal.

This is an important identity marker.

Do not replace it with:

- conventional tiara
- crown
- hair bow
- random gemstone
- flowers

---

## 5. Core Crystal

Pyra's glowing turquoise/teal crystal motifs are major visual anchors.

The central chest crystal should appear:

- luminous
- geometric
- turquoise/teal
- integrated into her armor rather than worn like ordinary jewelry

Additional teal illumination may appear throughout her outfit.

Glow should remain controlled rather than turning her entire costume neon.

---

## 6. Primary Outfit

Pyra wears a highly distinctive futuristic fantasy combat outfit combining armor, bodysuit elements, exposed sections, and glowing technological details.

### Primary Colors

1. Crimson / deep red
2. Black / extremely dark burgundy
3. Metallic gold
4. Turquoise / teal illumination

### Torso

The torso combines:

- sculpted red armor
- dark textured bodysuit material
- gold structural trim
- turquoise glowing seams
- central crystal ornament
- exposed waist sections

The armor follows her figure closely.

It should appear engineered and ornamental rather than resembling medieval plate armor.

### Shoulders

Angular red-and-gold armor elements frame the shoulders.

They should remain relatively compact and elegant.

Avoid enormous MMO-style pauldrons.

### Arms

Pyra uses asymmetrical futuristic armor and glove elements.

Typical details include:

- black fingerless glove material
- red armored sections
- gold trim
- turquoise crystal accents

### Waist / Hips

The design becomes more open around the hips.

Important elements include:

- narrow red structural straps/panels
- exposed upper thighs/hips
- gold fittings
- layered armor components
- long decorative trailing pieces

Do not convert this area into a conventional skirt.

### Legs

Signature leg treatment:

- exposed upper thighs
- tall red armored boots/greaves
- dark and gold structural details
- turquoise accents

### Footwear

Red armored heeled boots.

The heels should look integrated into a fantasy/sci-fi combat design rather than ordinary fashion pumps.

---

## 7. Back Design

The back is important for turnaround references.

Maintain:

- short red bob visible from behind
- red/gold upper-back armor
- prominent turquoise crystal detail
- exposed portions of the back
- structured hip armor
- long trailing red/black panels with gold and teal details
- tall red boots

Do not invent long hair to conceal difficult back details.

---

## 8. Trailing Panels

Long decorative armor/ribbon-like pieces extend behind Pyra from approximately the waist/back region.

They combine:

- crimson
- black
- gold edging
- teal luminous accents

They should feel like flexible fantasy-tech components rather than ordinary cloth ribbons.

Their exact position can change naturally with movement.

---

## 9. Weapon

Pyra uses an elaborate Aegis sword.

General visual language:

- oversized fantasy sword
- red dominant structure
- gold framework
- turquoise/teal crystal energy
- sharp geometric construction
- technologically advanced appearance
- flame associations

The weapon should feel designed from the same visual vocabulary as her armor.

Avoid generic medieval swords.

---

## 10. Elemental Theme

**Primary element: Fire**

Fire effects should favor:

- red
- orange
- gold
- white-hot highlights

Teal remains associated primarily with Aegis crystal technology rather than replacing her fire coloration.

---

## 11. Personality

Pyra is fundamentally:

- kind
- gentle
- caring
- patient
- considerate
- supportive
- quietly determined
- protective of people she cares about
- somewhat reserved compared with more boisterous personalities

Her kindness should feel sincere rather than naïve.

She is capable of fighting and making difficult decisions but does not need to behave aggressively to demonstrate strength.

---

## 12. Emotional Presentation

### Default Expression

Soft smile with attentive eye contact.

### Happy

- warm smile
- softened eyes
- subtle blush
- relaxed posture

### Cheerful

- brighter smile
- more energetic eyes
- slightly animated body language

### Embarrassed

- visible blush
- slightly averted gaze
- restrained smile or uncertain mouth
- gentle rather than exaggerated reaction

### Concerned

- softened eyebrows
- focused eyes
- restrained expression

### Determined

- direct gaze
- firm mouth
- upright posture
- calm confidence

### Combat

Focused and decisive.

Avoid transforming her into a permanently angry or bloodthirsty character.

---

## 13. Body Language

Pyra generally carries herself gracefully.

Preferred poses:

- relaxed standing posture
- hands gently held near torso
- one hand resting near chest
- friendly three-quarter stance
- subtle hip shift
- calm eye contact
- poised sword stance
- protective combat posture

Her curvy silhouette can remain visible without requiring exaggerated pin-up posing.

---

## 14. Rendering Guidance

Preferred visual treatments:

- premium modern anime illustration
- polished Japanese game key art
- detailed cel shading
- anime-CGI hybrid rendering
- crisp linework
- controlled glossy armor reflections
- luminous crystal effects
- clean facial rendering
- rich but disciplined saturation

Her armor benefits from visible differences between:

- polished metal
- textured dark bodysuit
- crystal
- skin
- flexible trailing components

---

## 15. Portrait Reference

For clean reference portraits:

**Composition:** bust or waist-up  
**Background:** pure white  
**Expression:** warm neutral smile  
**Camera:** eye level or slightly above  
**Pose:** front or gentle three-quarter view

Must clearly show:

- face
- red bob
- amber-red eyes
- gold/teal head ornament
- chest crystal
- upper armor
- shoulder armor

Avoid dramatic environmental lighting in reference portraits.

---

## 16. Full-Body Reference

For clean character references:

**Background:** pure white  
**Pose:** neutral standing  
**Camera:** approximately waist/chest height  
**Lens:** minimal perspective distortion  
**Framing:** entire character including footwear

Preserve realistic consistency between head, torso, legs, and footwear.

---

## 17. Turnaround / Triptych Standard

Recommended arrangement:

**FRONT | SIDE | BACK**

All three views must depict:

- the same character
- same proportions
- same hairstyle
- same costume
- same armor configuration
- same footwear
- same color palette

Use a white background and even studio/reference lighting.

Do not redesign costume elements between views.

---

## 18. Character-Sheet Standard

A comprehensive Pyra reference sheet should ideally contain:

- identity panel
- full-body front view
- full-body side view
- full-body back view
- head front
- head three-quarter
- head profile
- expression studies
- hair detail
- head ornament detail
- chest/core crystal detail
- glove/gauntlet detail
- boot detail
- torso armor detail
- back armor detail
- weapon reference
- color palette

Reference sheets should prioritize **consistency and clarity over dramatic composition**.

---

## 19. Critical Consistency Rules

Always preserve:

1. Short crimson-red bob.
2. Amber-red eyes.
3. Gold head ornament with teal crystal.
4. Curvaceous hourglass proportions.
5. Generous bust consistent with Pyra's established design.
6. Red/black/gold primary costume.
7. Teal luminous crystal technology.
8. Exposed waist and upper-thigh design.
9. Tall red armored boots.
10. Long trailing armor/ribbon elements.
11. Warm, gentle facial identity.
12. Fire elemental association.

---

## 20. Common Generation Errors

Avoid:

- long red hair
- ponytail
- generic fantasy armor
- medieval plate armor
- red eyes without amber/orange warmth
- blue eyes
- green eyes
- missing core crystal
- blue core crystal
- missing head ornament
- random crown
- overly bulky armor
- conventional skirt
- ordinary thigh-high stockings replacing armor
- generic high heels
- shrinking her proportions into an unrelated slender archetype
- extreme anatomical exaggeration
- inconsistent bust/hip proportions between views
- changing costume between turnaround panels
- excessive teal glow
- generic red-haired anime-girl facial drift
- making her permanently seductive, angry, or smug

**Avoid:** long hair, ponytail, blue or green eyes, missing head crystal, generic crown, generic red-haired anime girl, medieval armor, bulky armor, conventional skirt, ordinary thigh-high stockings, ordinary pumps, flat rectangular body proportions, excessively slender figure, extreme anatomical exaggeration, missing teal illumination, excessive neon glow, costume redesign, inconsistent turnaround anatomy, chibi proportions, childlike appearance.

---

## 23. Reference Priority

When visual references are supplied, use this priority:

**1. Approved Pyra portrait** — facial identity, hair, eyes, head ornament, rendering style.

**2. Approved full-body triptych** — body proportions, complete costume, footwear, back design, and turnaround consistency.

**3. Character sheet** — secondary costume details, expressions, equipment, color relationships, and accessory construction.

**4. This portable specification** — semantic guidance, personality, consistency rules, and correction of model drift.

When textual and visual details conflict, preserve recognizable canonical Pyra identity first and use the approved visual references to maintain continuity within the image set.
